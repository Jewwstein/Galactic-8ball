# Soul Slime HD Creator v0.1

Landscape-first Android character creator prototype for the Soul Slime HD/2.5D direction.

## Rendering
- The slime preview is rendered live in a 2048×2048 SubViewport.
- Character art is layered and procedural so body, eyes, core, markings, transparency and aura can be changed independently.
- The creator saves its configuration to `user://slime_creator.json`.
- SAVE LOOK also captures a 2048×2048 PNG preview internally.

## Customization
- 4 body silhouettes
- 6 palettes
- 4 eye styles
- 4 markings
- 4 core styles
- transparency
- aura intensity
- randomize

This prototype intentionally focuses on the creator before the HD cave tutorial is rebuilt.
